Add lifecycle segmentation and follow-up email automation to RecruitCandidates so every company signup is grouped based on how far they have progressed toward publishing a job and using the platform.

The goal is to build a warm audience from people who create accounts but do not subscribe immediately, then follow up with messages that match their exact stage instead of sending everyone the same email.

Create these lifecycle segments:

* Signed up but did not complete company setup
* Completed company setup but did not create a job
* Created a draft job but did not subscribe
* Subscribed but did not publish a job
* Published a job but has no applications
* Receiving applications
* Reached plan limit

A user or company should always belong to the most relevant current segment. Their segment should update automatically whenever they complete an important action.

Use the company workspace as the main lifecycle record, not just the individual user account, because multiple team members may belong to the same company.

Track these events:

* Account created
* Email verified
* Company profile completed
* Logo uploaded
* Career page previewed
* First draft job created
* Subscription started
* Subscription activated
* First job published
* First application received
* Application allowance reached
* Subscription cancelled
* Subscription payment failed

Store the current lifecycle segment on the company and also maintain a lifecycle-event history so movement between segments can be reviewed later.

The segment logic should work like this:

### Signed up but did not complete company setup

Conditions:

* Account and company workspace exist
* Company profile is incomplete
* No job has been created

Recommended follow-up:

> Complete your company profile and get your recruitment page ready.

CTA:

> Finish company setup

### Completed company setup but did not create a job

Conditions:

* Company profile is complete
* No draft or published job exists

Recommended follow-up:

> Your company page is ready. Create your first role to start collecting applications.

CTA:

> Create your first job

### Created a draft job but did not subscribe

Conditions:

* At least one draft job exists
* No active paid subscription
* No published job exists

This is the highest-intent unpaid segment and should receive the strongest follow-up.

Use this message direction:

**Subject:** Your role is still saved in RecruitCandidates

**Body:**

> Your role is still saved in RecruitCandidates. Choose a plan to publish it and start receiving applications through your company’s recruitment page.

CTA:

> Publish your role

The CTA should take them directly to the saved draft or billing step required to publish it.

### Subscribed but did not publish

Conditions:

* Active subscription exists
* At least one draft job exists or no published job exists
* No active published role

Recommended follow-up:

> Your workspace is active. Publish your first role and start receiving candidates.

CTA:

> Publish your first role

### Published but has no applications

Conditions:

* At least one published job exists
* Total applications are zero

Recommended follow-up:

> Your job is live. Share your application link or career page to start receiving candidates.

CTA options:

* Copy application link
* View career page

### Receiving applications

Conditions:

* At least one application exists
* Company is below its plan limit

This is an active customer segment.

Do not send basic onboarding reminders.

Use this segment for:

* Product education
* Screening tips
* Assessment and video interview guidance
* Usage summaries
* Feature adoption messages

### Reached plan limit

Conditions:

* Application usage has reached the allowed plan capacity, including purchased add-ons and grace usage

Recommended follow-up:

> Your company has reached its current application allowance. Buy more applications or upgrade your plan to keep receiving candidates.

CTA options:

* Buy more applications
* Upgrade plan

Do not mention plan limits in candidate-facing emails or public job pages.

Add a lifecycle automation system that can send emails based on both segment and inactivity.

Suggested timing:

* Signed up but incomplete setup: 24 hours after signup
* Setup complete but no job: 24 hours after setup completion
* Draft job but not subscribed: 2 hours after draft creation, then 24 hours later, then 72 hours later
* Subscribed but not published: 6 hours after subscription activation, then 24 hours later
* Published but no applications: 24 hours after publishing, then 72 hours later
* Reached plan limit: immediately, then once again after 24 hours if unresolved

Do not send repeated reminders after the user completes the required action. Cancel or skip pending messages as soon as the company moves into another segment.

Use Resend for lifecycle emails.

Store the Resend ID for scheduled emails so pending reminders can be cancelled when the company progresses.

Create a lifecycle email log containing:

* company_id
* user_id or recipient
* lifecycle segment
* email type
* subject
* status
* resend_id
* scheduled_for
* sent_at
* cancelled_at
* failure reason
* created_at

Avoid sending multiple lifecycle emails to different team members in the same company. By default, send onboarding and billing-related messages to the company owner.

Allow platform admins to inspect:

* Current segment per company
* Time spent in each segment
* Last lifecycle email sent
* Next scheduled email
* Companies with draft jobs but no subscription
* Companies subscribed but not published
* Companies with live jobs but no applications
* Companies at or near usage limits

Add simple filters in the platform admin for every segment.

Create internal analytics for:

* Signups
* Company setup completion
* Draft jobs created
* Paid subscriptions
* Jobs published
* First applications received
* Conversion from signup to draft job
* Conversion from draft job to paid subscription
* Conversion from subscription to published role
* Conversion from published role to first application

The most important funnel should be visible as:

**Signup → Company setup → Draft job → Subscription → Published job → First application**

Treat “draft job created but not subscribed” as the highest-priority warm audience because they have already invested effort and shown clear buying intent.

Do not use a separate marketing platform for the first version. Keep the segmentation, automation and reporting inside RecruitCandidates using the existing database and Resend setup.

Keep the implementation aligned with the current onboarding flow:

* Users can create and explore their workspace before subscribing
* Users can create a draft job before subscribing
* Subscription is required when they try to publish the job
* After payment, return them to the saved draft and make publishing the next clear action

Do not change unrelated product features or redesign the dashboard beyond what is needed to support lifecycle tracking and follow-up emails.
Add internal funnel tracking for completed signups, company setup completion, first draft job creation, subscription activation, first published job and first application received. Show total counts, conversion percentages and drop-off between these stages in the platform admin.
